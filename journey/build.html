02 THE BUILD

From an idea
to a working
prototype.

The early days of SAKHAA — choosing components, building the first system, dealing with failures and getting the wheelchair to actually move.

01

STARTING FROM AN IDEA

The idea was simple.

What if a wheelchair could do more than simply move?

That question became the starting point for SAKHAA. We wanted to explore how technology could be brought together to create a wheelchair that could support greater independence while also understanding more about its surroundings.

But an idea on paper is very different from a system that actually works.

Turning SAKHAA into a physical prototype meant figuring out how the different parts could work together as one system.

02

BUILDING THE SYSTEM

Many components. One system.

The prototype brought together hardware, sensors, control systems and software. Each part had a role, but none of them could work in isolation.

01

Control

The system needed a controller capable of receiving inputs and coordinating the wheelchair.

02

Movement

Motors and motor drivers formed the foundation of the physical movement system.

03

Sensing

Sensors gave the system information about obstacles, movement and orientation.

04

Communication

Connectivity opened the possibility of linking the wheelchair with software and caregiver systems.

THE EARLY PROTOTYPE

The first versions were about understanding how the individual pieces could become one working system.

03

THE REALITY OF BUILDING

It didn't work the first time.

Building a prototype is rarely a straight line.

Components have to be tested. Connections have to be checked. Systems have to communicate correctly. A change in one part can affect something somewhere else.

There were moments when the system behaved differently from what we expected.

Those failures became part of the process. Every problem gave us another thing to understand, test and improve.

BUILD. TEST. FAIL. FIX. REPEAT.
04

BRINGING IT TOGETHER

The prototype started to feel real.

The biggest shift came when the individual experiments started becoming one connected system.

Movement, sensing and control were no longer separate experiments. They were becoming parts of the same wheelchair.

This was also where the project started moving beyond a collection of components. We were beginning to see how hardware and software could work together around a real user.

05

THE FIRST MOVEMENT

An idea moved.

There is something different about seeing an idea move for the first time.

Until that moment, SAKHAA existed mostly as diagrams, components, code and conversations.

When the prototype finally responded and moved, the project became tangible in a completely different way.

06

WHAT BUILDING TAUGHT US

The prototype became the teacher.

Building SAKHAA taught us things that could not be understood completely from research papers or component datasheets.

01

Integration matters.

Individual components only become useful when they work together as one system.

02

Testing changes ideas.

Real hardware reveals problems that are difficult to see on paper.

03

Failures are part of building.

Every failure gave us another opportunity to understand what needed to change.

THE BUILD

The prototype was only the beginning.

From components and experiments to a system that could actually move.
