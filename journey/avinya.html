04 / AVINYA HACKATHON C. V. RAMAN GLOBAL UNIVERSITY

Taking SAKHAA to the next stage.

From a working prototype to a hackathon winner — AVINYA pushed us to think beyond what we had already built.

01

THE HACKATHON

32 teams.
One competition.

The AVINYA Hackathon was conducted at C. V. Raman Global University.

The preliminary round witnessed participation from 32 teams, each bringing their own ideas, prototypes and approaches to the competition.

For us, it was another opportunity to put SAKHAA in front of people outside our usual environment and see how it stood up against other projects.

02

THE EVALUATION

Only 8
made it through.

The preliminary round was followed by an intense technical evaluation.

After the evaluation, only 8 teams managed to qualify for the next stage.

32 TEAMS PARTICIPATED
08 TEAMS QUALIFIED
03

THE FINAL STAGE

Qualifying was
only the beginning.

Making it into the final group meant that we now had to present SAKHAA at another level.

We had to explain the problem, demonstrate the prototype and communicate why the idea mattered.

The experience pushed us to look beyond the prototype itself and think about what SAKHAA could become as a real product.

AVINYA HACKATHON FINAL RESULT

WINNER

SAKHAA

Declared winner among the 8 teams that qualified through the technical evaluation.

04

WHAT IT MEANT

More than
another win.

AVINYA was an important moment for SAKHAA.

We had started building the project in our college environment. Now, it had been evaluated alongside other teams and their ideas.

Winning gave us confidence that the idea could stand outside our own circle and make an impact when presented to a wider audience.

But the biggest takeaway was simple: there was still a lot more to build.

AVINYA HACKATHON

The prototype was getting bigger.

What started as a college project was slowly becoming something we wanted to take further.
