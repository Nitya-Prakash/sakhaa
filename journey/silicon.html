03 / TECHTRONICS 2026

PROTOTYPE EXHIBITION · SENIOR DIVISION

Building
under pressure.

Two sleepless nights. One working prototype. And an exhibition we never expected to win.

01

THE EVENT

We weren't
thinking about winning.

The event was called Prototype Exhibition (Senior Division) at TECHTRONICS 2026.

There were so many well-built projects around us. Looking at what other teams had brought, honestly, winning wasn't even something we were thinking about.

We were just trying to make SAKHAA work.

02

THE TWO NIGHTS BEFORE

Build.
Test.
Repeat.

The two nights before the exhibition were some of the hardest we had worked on SAKHAA.

We barely slept.

We were constantly testing, fixing things, changing things and trying to get the prototype working — even if only at a beginner level.

It wasn't polished. It wasn't perfect. But we were determined to make it work.

THE GOAL

Make the prototype work.

03

THE EXHIBITION

And then people started stopping.

Once the exhibition started, something changed.

People came over to see the prototype. They asked questions. They looked at the wheelchair. They wanted to understand what we were building.

The prototype we had spent those sleepless nights putting together was getting attention.

People liked what they saw.

04 / THE MOMENT

We weren't
expecting it.

There were so many strong projects at the event. We never went into the exhibition thinking that SAKHAA would win.

We were simply proud that the prototype was working and that people were interested in it.

And then the winner was announced.

TECHTRONICS 2026 PROTOTYPE EXHIBITION

WINNER

SAKHAA

Two sleepless nights suddenly felt worth it.

05

WHAT WE LEARNED

It didn't have
to be perfect.

SAKHAA was still a prototype. It wasn't a finished product and there was still a lot left to improve.

But it was working.

And people could see what we were trying to build. They could understand the idea because the prototype was there in front of them.

Sometimes, you don't need to have everything figured out to build something people believe in.

TECHTRONICS 2026

The prototype spoke for itself.

Two sleepless nights, one working prototype, and a win we never expected.
