01 / WHAT IS SAKHAA?

A wheelchair for more independent living.

For someone with limited mobility, getting from one place to another can often mean depending on someone else.

SAKHAA is an intelligent wheelchair designed to make everyday mobility more independent, safer and more accessible.

Built with affordability at its core, SAKHAA aims to bring smarter mobility to the people who need it.

02 / WHY SAKHAA EXISTS?

A wheelchair can help someone move.

But movement alone isn't independence.

Because independence isn't simply about reaching a destination. It's about having the freedom to choose how you get there.

Where SAKHAA
